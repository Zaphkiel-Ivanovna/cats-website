**Model Scaling** does not scale the model by itself. It shows the interface of **Immersive Scaler**, a separate add-on made for resizing avatars to a target height and fixing their proportions for VR.

## Install Immersive Scaler

The panel tells you what it found:

| Message                                | What to do                                                                                                |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Immersive Scaler is not installed!** | Click **Download Immersive Scaler**, install the zip from its releases page as an add-on, then come back. |
| **Immersive Scaler is not enabled!**   | Click **Enable Immersive Scaler**. Cats turns off older copies and enables the current one.               |

Cats needs Immersive Scaler 0.5.2 or newer. Once it is installed and enabled, its controls appear directly in this panel.

## Use it

The controls belong to Immersive Scaler, so its own guide is the reference: click **Immersive Scaler Help** at the top of the panel to open it.

<Aside type="tip">
	Scale the avatar before you create eye tracking or visemes, and before you export. Changing the
	size afterwards can shift eye bones and shape keys.
</Aside>
